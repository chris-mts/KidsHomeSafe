The Simple Strategy

  1. 1

    Turn on Google SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode on every kid device.

  2. 2

    Configure a free DNS filter (like CleanBrowsing) on your router so the whole house is protected.

  3. 3

    Add a paid parental-control app (like Bark) on each kid’s phone for detailed monitoring.

  4. 4

    Use Apple Screen Time or Google Family Link to control app installs and downtime.
