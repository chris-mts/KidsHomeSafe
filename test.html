Web filters for families

These tools help you block adult content, malware, and other harmful sites before they ever load. Start with a free DNS filter, then add a paid parental-control app if you want deeper monitoring.

The simple strategy

  1. Turn on Google SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode on every kid device.
  2. Configure a free DNS filter on your router so the whole house is protected.
  3. Add a paid parental-control app on each kid’s phone or tablet for app-level monitoring.
  4. Use Apple Screen Time or Google Family Link to control app installs and screen time.
