Parental-control tool reviews

Real-world breakdowns of the tools I recommend to parents. I focus on clarity, safety, and ease of use — not just fancy features.

Bark Review – AI monitoring for busy parents

Bark connects to your child’s devices and accounts, scans content using AI, and sends alerts when there are signs of bullying, self-harm, explicit content, or predators.

  • Best for: Parents with older kids active on social media
  • Strengths: Smart alerts, wide app coverage
  • Weaknesses: Setup can be a bit technical on some platforms
